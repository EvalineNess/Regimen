# Regimen
💊 Regimen
A lifestyle advisor for people taking one or more medications

Type in what you're on, answer a few relevant questions, 
get personalized advice — instead of reading a drug label's tiny print.

✨ What it does

Regimen guides you toward the best use of your medications. 
Instead of reading a drug label's full text — printed so small it's a chore to get through — 
all it takes is typing in the drug name, answering a few relevant questions, and getting advice made for you.

🔄 How it works :


💊 Add your medications one at a time. Each one is looked up live against RxNorm (NIH/NLM) to resolve the generic ingredient and drug class, then against openFDA to pull the actual FDA-approved label.

❓ Answer a few questions , the app scans each label for 12 lifestyle-relevant patterns and only asks about what's actually relevant to what you're taking: 🍽️ food timing · ☕ caffeine · 🍷 alcohol · 🍊 grapefruit · 🚬 smoking · 🚗 driving/sedation · ☀️ sun exposure · 💧 hydration · 💫 dizziness on standing · 🥛 dairy/calcium · ⏰ time-of-day dosing · 🤰 pregnancy/breastfeeding

✅ Get your advice : your answers are compared against the label text and grouped by medicine, showing what you're already doing right and what needs adjusting.
